## Product Overview

**One-liner:** The first Texas-personalized travel hacking platform that creates safe, automated trip plans based on your airport, your budget, and your dream destinations.

**What it does:** Texas Cheap Flights is a personalized travel-hacking and flight-optimization platform built specifically for Texans. It helps users find the cheapest flights from Texas airports, maximize credit card points responsibly, and generate fully automated trip plans based on their dream destinations. The platform uses AI, Texas-specific travel patterns, and safe credit card strategies to map out the fastest, most affordable path to any vacation.

**Product category:** Travel Optimization Platform
**Subcategories:** Flight Deal Intelligence · Award Travel Strategy · Credit Card Optimization

**Product type:** SaaS / Content Platform (web-first, mobile planned)

**Business model:** Freemium (free tools + affiliate revenue; membership tier planned in 12-month roadmap)

## Target Audience

**Target users:**
- Texas travelers of all experience levels
- Beginners & intermediates in travel hacking (primary focus)
- Budget-conscious families planning domestic & international trips
- Vacationers flying from regional Texas airports (MFE, BRO, HRL, LRD)
- Texans wanting premium travel for cheap (DFW, AUS, IAH hubs)

**Decision-maker:** The individual traveler or household budget-manager

**Primary use case:** Find the cheapest way to fly from a specific Texas airport to a dream destination using points, credit card strategy, and award booking.

**Jobs to be done:**
1. "Find me the cheapest way to fly from my Texas airport to my dream destination." (Deals, sweet spots, partner awards, cash vs points arbitrage)
2. "Build me a safe, step-by-step credit card strategy that matches my travel goals." (No risky tactics, no overwhelm, full automation)
3. "Show me the smartest way to book my trip — cash or points — and protect me with hedged backup options." (Refundable awards, backup itineraries, positioning flights)

**Specific use cases:**
- Regional TX traveler (e.g., MFE) doing a positioning flight to DFW to catch cheap international award
- Family pooling points across a household for a group trip to Orlando
- Beginner earning a 60K SUB and redeeming for Cancun
- Premium traveler booking DFW→Tokyo via ANA partner award

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Most Texans overpay for flights because they don't know the cheapest routes, partner award availability, or the best credit card strategies tied to their home airport.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- **TPG / blogs**: Generic advice, heavily affiliate-biased, not personalized to airport or goal
- **AwardWallet**: Tracks points but gives zero strategy or planning
- **Airline sites**: Only show their own inventory; miss partner award space
- **JGOOT**: Simplified but outdated, not personalized, no automation
- **Reddit/FlyerTalk**: Valuable but overwhelming, unstructured, requires expertise

**What it costs them:**
- Paying 3–10x more in cash for flights they could book with points
- Earning points on the wrong cards (no category optimization)
- Transferring points to the wrong program (irreversible mistakes)
- Missing ideal award space from lack of partner knowledge

**Emotional tension:**
- "I know this is out there but it feels too complicated for me."
- "I'm scared of messing up my credit score."
- "I don't know if I'm getting a good deal or not."
- "I feel like I'm leaving money on the table every time I fly."

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors:**
- **The Points Guy (TPG)** — same audience, massive SEO, but heavily affiliate-biased, no personalization, generic content
- **JGOOT** — simplified travel hacking, but outdated, not Texas-specific, no automation

**Secondary competitors:**
- **AwardWallet** — point tracking only, no strategy or planning layer
- **ExpertFlyer / Point.me / AwardHacker** — power tools for advanced users only, steep learning curve

**Indirect competitors:**
- Google Flights / Kayak / Hopper — cash booking tools, ignore points entirely
- Generic credit card comparison sites — no travel-goal alignment

**How each falls short:**
- None are Texas-first or airport-specific
- None pair credit card strategy with award booking in one flow
- None teach "travel hedging" as a distinct strategy
- None offer real-time, personalized deal scoring

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
1. **Texas-first, airport-specific personalization** (MFE, DFW, AUS, IAH, HRL, BRO, LRD)
2. **Travel hedging as a proprietary category** (backup itineraries, refundable awards, positioning flights — rarely taught)
3. **Safe, responsible credit strategy** embedded throughout (no MS, no rapid churn)
4. **Goal-first planning engine** (Dream Trip → Card → Points → Book → Hedge)
5. **Transparency over affiliate bias** — clear risk ratings and educational-only labels for advanced tactics

**How we do it differently:** We start with the traveler's dream destination and home airport, then work backwards to build a personalized, automated plan — rather than surfacing generic deals.

**Why that's better:** Users get a step-by-step path tailored to their exact situation rather than having to filter through generic, biased content.

**Why customers choose us:** "It's the only tool that actually knows where I fly from and builds a plan just for me."

**How they describe us (aspirational):**

- "It just tells me exactly what to do."
- "I finally feel like I'm not leaving money on the table."

**Words/phrases to USE:**
- "Your airport" / "from [city]"
- "Dream trip"
- "Safe" / "step-by-step"
- "Points you already have"
- "Cheapest path"
- "Partner award" / "sweet spot"
- "Hedge your trip"
- "Cents per point (CPP)"

**Words/phrases to AVOID:**
- "Churning" (sounds manipulative)
- "Manufactured spending" (risky/illegal-adjacent)
- "Hack your bank" (alarming)
- "Unlimited" (overpromising)
- "Guaranteed deals" (inaccurate)

**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SUB | Sign-Up Bonus — one-time points bonus after meeting minimum spend |
| CPP | Cents Per Point — how much each point is worth in cash terms |
| Saver Award | Fixed low-cost award ticket with limited availability |
| Partner Award | Book one airline's flight using another airline's miles |
| Positioning Flight | Cheap flight to a hub to catch a better international award |
| Travel Hedging | Booking refundable backups + diversifying programs to reduce risk |
| Sweet Spot | A route/hotel where awards are priced far below their cash value |
| 5/24 | Chase rule: denied if you've opened 5+ cards in 24 months |
