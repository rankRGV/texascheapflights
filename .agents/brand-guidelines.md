## Brand Essence

**Mission:** Make premium travel accessible to every Texan — safely, smartly, and without the bias.

**Personality:** Trustworthy expert friend. Texas-proud. No jargon. No hype.

**Tagline:**
> "Most travel hackers teach you how to earn points. We teach you how to use them without getting burned."

**Short tagline (for social/logo):**
> "Travel hacking built for Texans."

## Logo

### Primary Logo Mark
- **Icon:** 5-pointed Texas Lone Star with airplane silhouette integrated
- **Wordmark:** TEXAS CHEAP FLIGHTS — Inter Black / Extra Bold, all caps
- **Lockup:** Icon left, wordmark right (horizontal) OR icon top, wordmark below (stacked)

### Logo Variants
| Variant | Use Case |
|---|---|
| Light (on white/light bg) | Print, light UI, email headers |
| Dark (on navy bg) | Website, social, dark UI |
| Icon only (star + plane) | Favicon, app icon, social avatar |

### Logo Rules
- Never stretch or distort the logo
- Never place on a busy photo without an overlay
- Minimum clear space: equal to the height of the "T" in TEXAS on all sides
- Minimum size: 120px wide for digital, 1 inch for print

## Typography

### Font Family
**Inter** (Google Fonts) — primary typeface for all text.

Fallback stack: `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif`

### Type Scale
| Role | Weight | Size | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Hero | Black (900) | 56–80px | Hero headlines only |
| H1 | ExtraBold (800) | 40–56px | Page titles |
| H2 | Bold (700) | 32–40px | Section headers |
| H3 | SemiBold (600) | 20–24px | Card titles, feature names |
| Body Large | Regular (400) | 18–20px | Lead paragraphs, subheads |
| Body | Regular (400) | 15–16px | Main body copy |
| Small / Label | Medium (500) | 12–13px | Labels, badges, captions |
| Micro | Regular (400) | 11px | Legal text, footnotes |

### Type Rules
- Headlines: tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`)
- Body: normal tracking (`0`)
- ALL CAPS: labels, badges, section categories only (never body copy)
- Line height: 1.2 for headlines, 1.6–1.7 for body

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Personality (5 adjectives)
1. **Texas-proud** — specific, local, knows your airport
2. **Trustworthy** — never pushes you into risk without flagging it
3. **Empowering** — "you can do this" energy, not gatekeeping
4. **Direct** — gets to the point, no filler
5. **Transparent** — affiliate disclosure visible, risk ratings everywhere

### Writing Rules
- Write like a smart friend, not a financial institution
- Short sentences. Active voice. No jargon without explanation.
- Use second person ("you," "your airport," "your dream trip")
- No exclamation points in body copy (save for headlines only, sparingly)
- Mirror customer language (see product-marketing-context.md)

### Words to USE
`your airport` · `dream trip` · `safe` · `step-by-step` · `hedge` · `sweet spot` · `partner award` · `points you already have`

### Words to AVOID
`churning` · `manufactured spending` · `hack your bank` · `unlimited` · `guaranteed deals` · `innovative` · `streamline`

## UI Patterns

### Cards
- Background: `rgba(255,255,255,0.04)` on dark / `#ffffff` on light
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` on dark
- Border radius: `16px` (2xl)
- Padding: `24–32px`

### Buttons
| Type | Style |
|---|---|
| Primary CTA | Gold gradient, navy text, rounded-full |
| Secondary | Outlined, gold border, gold text |
| Ghost | Transparent, white text |

### Badges / Pills
- Category labels: ALL CAPS, 11-12px, gold color on dark bg, rounded-full
- Strategy ratings: colored dot + label (green/amber/red)

### Forms
- Input background: `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`
- Focus border: gold `rgba(212,168,67,0.6)`
- Focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(212,168,67,0.12)`

### Spacing
Base unit: `4px`. Use multiples of 4: `8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px`

## Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Do
- Lead with the user's specific airport
- Show the dream destination early
- Give every strategy a safety rating
- Disclose affiliate relationships clearly
- Use Texas-specific examples (MFE, DFW, IAH) not generic (JFK)

### ❌ Don't
- Recommend manufactured spending or rapid churn
- Use dark patterns to pressure card applications
- Bury affiliate disclosure in fine print
- Use generic travel stock photography (no beaches with cocktails)
- Make financial promises or guarantees
